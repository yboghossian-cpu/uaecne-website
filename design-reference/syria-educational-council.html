Overview

The Highest Body for Our Schools

The Educational Council of the Armenian Evangelical Community of Syria is the highest body responsible for the community’s education. It shapes educational policy, sets strategic direction, and oversees its schools — six in all: four in Aleppo, one in Damascus, and one in Kessab. Together these schools carry forward an educational mission more than a century old.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Mission

To ensure a high standard of education grounded in Christian, national, and universal human values — raising conscious, responsible, capable, and service-minded generations, ready to contribute to the community and to the life of the homeland.

Vision

To create a modern, safe, and inspiring educational environment in which knowledge, moral values, creative thinking, and social responsibility grow — strengthening the community’s schools as pillars of Syria’s educational and cultural life.

What the Council Does

Four Areas of Work

Academic Oversight

Setting educational policy and strategy, monitoring teaching quality, improving curricula, and developing teachers.

Administration

Keeping the schools running well, and coordinating buildings, materials, and technical needs.

Financial Management

Financial planning and oversight, and the effective distribution of resources across the schools.

Institutional Development

Long-term strategy, and strengthening the educational, cultural, and social role of the schools.

One Council, Six Schools, One Mission

Together, these six institutions continue the Armenian Evangelical Community of Syria’s educational mission of more than a century — raising conscious, educated generations who keep their national identity and are guided by Christian values. The Council serves them all, within the Union of the Armenian Evangelical Churches in the Near East.
